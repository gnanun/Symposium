The Symposium

Symposium 2.0 is the second edition of our department's annual national-level technical symposium. Built on the success of Edition 1, this year's event brings together students for a full day of competitive programming, hardware challenges, and idea-pitching events, alongside talks and hands-on sessions.

The symposium is designed as an ongoing platform — each year adds a new edition, while everything from previous years stays archived and visible, so the event's history and growth are always on record.

Shridevi Institute of Engineering and Technology

Founded in 2002 by the Sri Shridevi Charitable Trust under the leadership of Chairman Dr. M. R. Hulinaykar, SIET is based in Tumakuru, Karnataka, and is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and approved by AICTE, New Delhi.

Over more than two decades, SIET has grown from an undergraduate-only institution into one offering 7 undergraduate programmes, 4 postgraduate programmes, and 4 PhD research programmes — including Computer Science & Engineering, AI & Data Science, Electronics & Communication, Civil, Electrical & Electronics, and Mechanical Engineering. The institute is actively engaged in the NAAC accreditation process through its Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC).

SIET's stated mission centres on providing quality technical education that balances strong theoretical foundations with practical, industry-ready skills — and encourages exactly the kind of student-led technical initiative that Symposium 2.0 represents.

Department of Computer Science & Engineering

The Department of CSE has been part of SIET since the institute's founding in 2002, making it one of the college's original departments. It has grown substantially since then — from an initial intake of 60 seats per year to 120 today, reflecting sustained demand and growth.

The department offers the full academic pathway in Computer Science: undergraduate (B.E.), postgraduate (M.Tech), and PhD research programmes, all following the VTU curriculum. Its active research areas include Computer Networks, Image Processing, and Data Mining.

In line with the department's mission — combining strong analytical and technical grounding with ethics, teamwork, and real-world readiness — the department supports Creative Codex in organising Symposium 2.0 each year, providing faculty guidance and institutional backing for the event.

Creative Codex

Creative Codex is the official technical club of the Department of CSE, SIET. The club plans and runs Symposium 2.0 end-to-end — from event design and registrations to on-ground execution — with a student team leading the effort each year, guided by faculty coordinators.

Club leadership changes every year, and this website is built so each new team can take over content and event management without needing to rebuild anything from scratch.
