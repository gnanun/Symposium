Travel & Accommodation

Getting to SIET, Tumakuru, and where to stay if you're travelling from outside the city.

Demo content — real travel and stay details to be added via the admin panel closer to the event

By Train

Tumakuru railway station is well-connected to Bengaluru and other major cities in Karnataka. Full timing and connection details will be added here.

By Road

SIET is easily reachable by road from Bengaluru (approx. 70 km). KSRTC buses run frequently between Bengaluru and Tumakuru.
